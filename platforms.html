Knowledge of Design

Design as a system of knowledge

KoD is a Rukun-led initiative examining how design creates, connects, and applies knowledge across science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics. Founded by Brandie Janow, it includes original research, publications, international sessions, workshops, partnerships, and an annual Riyadh edition.
