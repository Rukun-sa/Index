Kilmitain Riyadh

Rukun partnered with Kilmitain and its founder, Sheikha Fouz Al-Sabah, on the forum's first Saudi edition. The Riyadh program brought together creative founders, investors, institutions, and decision-makers through strategic discussions, masterclasses, venture showcases, advisory sessions, and professional development.

AlMashtal Creative Labs & Makers Lab

Design Awakening

A product-design pathway from education into professional practice

Design Awakening is a studio-based residency for early-career Saudi product designers, developed around real materials, suppliers, production constraints, prototyping, professional documentation, and exhibition-ready output. The program combines AlMashtal's making infrastructure with an ESDi curriculum partnership.
