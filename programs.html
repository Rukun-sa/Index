Creative Women Forum

HRH Princess Noura is the official patron of the Creative Women Forum in Saudi Arabia, with Rukun serving as a partner to its Saudi editions. The relationship also extends to Creative Women's annual London program.

Kilmitain Riyadh

Rukun partnered with Kilmitain and its founder, Sheikha Fouz Al-Sabah, to bring the forum to Saudi Arabia for the first time. The Riyadh edition brought together creative founders, investors, institutions, and decision-makers through strategic discussions, training, venture showcases, advisory sessions, and professional development.
