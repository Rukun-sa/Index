HRH Princess Noura bint Saud bin Naif Al Saud

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Her Royal Highness Princess Noura bint Saud bin Naif Al Saud is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Rukun and Co-Founder of AlMashtal. With a background in art, design, and media, she studied in London before completing a Master's in Contemporary Design at Sotheby's Institute of Art and an Executive Leadership Program at THNK School of Creative Leadership in Amsterdam.

Since 2013, she has worked across Saudi Arabia's cultural and creative sectors, developing initiatives that connect creativity with strategy, entrepreneurship, cultural production, and economic opportunity. Her work focuses on strengthening the systems, access, and opportunities that allow creative talent to grow and contribute to Saudi Arabia's cultural future.

Dr. Gjoko Muratovski

Designer and innovation consultant

Dr. Muratovski is an award-winning designer and innovation consultant who has advised universities, Fortune 500 companies, and governments worldwide. His international thought-leadership experience includes the European Commission, Forbes Councils, Stanford's Institute for Innovation in Developing Economies, the Oxford Digital Leaders Network, the Yale Higher Education Leadership Summit, and the White House Presidential Innovation Fellows Program.

He served as the inaugural Ullman Endowed Chair and Director of the Ullman School of Design and currently serves as Chief Innovation Officer at ICONA, an Italian automotive design consultancy.

Dr. Deaa Bataineh

Designer, researcher, advisor, and professor

Dr. Bataineh's expertise combines design, strategy, and systems. His research examines how decisions are made across energy, mobility, health, food, public infrastructure, EV charging networks, cashless payment systems, and emergency response. He holds a PhD in Design from the Institute of Design, where his research advanced Strategic Choice-Making as a form of know-how for difficult organizational and societal questions.

He is Deputy Director of the Center of Excellence for Innovative Projects at Jordan University of Science and Technology and Professor of Design and Innovative Strategies. His international experience includes talks, workshops, consultations, and design services for organizations including the UNDP, European Union, and UN Global Compact.

Brandie Janow

Strategy and Cultural Development Advisor, Rukun

Brandie Janow is a researcher, strategist, futurist, and advisor whose work advances innovation at the intersection of design, culture, and creative economies. Her research has concentrated on the Saudi creative sector, with a focus on the design industry and its development.

She studied Visual Communication Design at the Art Institute in Chicago and later Speculative Design and Futurism at Parsons School of Design. She is the founder of Knowledge of Design and Kingdom Creatives, serves on the Iowa State University College of Design Advisory Board, and was selected for the Business Council for the Arts' 2026–2027 Leadership Arts Institute in Dallas.
