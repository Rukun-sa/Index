Positioning

Ideas shaped by place.

Place means more than geography. It includes the culture, history, people, materials, environment, institutions, and local knowledge of a specific context, and how that context has developed over time. Rukun works from the particulars of a place rather than applying one model everywhere.

Institution

Rukun

Rukun is a cultural systems and infrastructure design company. Its role is to design the structures behind creative sectors, from program architecture and institutional models to ecosystem frameworks and capacity systems.
