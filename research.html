Cross-program synthesis

Creative-Sector Signals

Recurring questions across research, programs, and audience evidence

Across the current evidence base, recurring questions concern the transition from education into professional practice, funding and market access, production capability, mentorship and professional networks, investment readiness, and how knowledge and outcomes are documented over time. Program feedback also repeatedly asks for deeper applied workshops, more structured networking, and clearer routes between creative founders, institutions, and investors.
